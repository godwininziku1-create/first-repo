Digi Girls

Practical digital skills and mentorship for girls and young women — learn, build and connect with mentors and internships.

Overview

Digi Girls is a cohort-based program delivering hands-on digital training, mentorship pairing and project showcases to create pathways into internships and entry-level roles.

What you learn

  • Device literacy & productivity tools
  • Intro to web development (HTML/CSS)
  • Basic programming logic (Python fundamentals)
  • Online safety and digital citizenship

Format

10-week cohort with weekly labs, mentorship sessions and a final demo day.

Labs

Hands-on small projects to build a portfolio.

Mentorship

Pairings and career coaching.

Demo Day

Showcase projects to partners and mentors.
